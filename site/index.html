An output of the NCCR Sisslerfeld project, developed by the Urban Energy Systems Lab, Empa. Explore how the energy system of the Sisslerfeld area behaves today and under different assumptions for industry, population, PV, heating, mobility and carbon price.

About this page

This is a static, read-only copy of the digital twin. All figures are pre-computed with the Empa energy-hub model (ehubX) and published here; the page runs entirely in your browser and needs no server. Detailed model inputs (technology and cost parameters, company-level data) are not part of this copy.
